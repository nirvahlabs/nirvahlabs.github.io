Privacy notice

Last updated: 25 October 2026. One notice for the Unnati app, this website and the free browser demo. Detailed app policy: Android app privacy policy.

The app

No account or login. Your answers, progress, notes, name and exam choice are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. There are no ads, analytics or tracking SDKs. Purchases are handled by Google Play, which shares with us only the order and licence information needed to unlock your exam. If Android backup is on, Android may copy the app's data to your own Google backup so it can move to a new phone. A backup file you export is saved where you choose; keep it private.

Reports you send

When you choose to report a problem, the app opens your own e-mail app with your message, the screen name, your exam, app version and device type, and a screenshot of that screen. Nothing is sent unless you press send. We use reports only to fix content and reply to you, and delete them when no longer needed.

This website

The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. Like any website, our hosting provider may record standard server logs (IP address, browser, time of request) for security and operation; we do not use them to identify or track visitors.

The free browser demo

The demo keeps your optional name, designation, exam and practice progress in your browser's local storage, on your own device only; nothing is sent to us. To remove it, clear this site's data in your browser settings (on a shared computer, do this after use).

Your choices and contact

Delete all app data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage, and demo data by clearing site data. For any privacy question or request, write to nirvahlabs@gmail.com.
