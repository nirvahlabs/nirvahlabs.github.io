How we check our answers

A wrong figure costs marks, so every question in Unnati is built and re-checked the same way.

1. Only official sources

Questions are written from public official sources: SBI's website, RBI directions and circulars, Acts on India Code, and the websites of NABARD, SIDBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA and government ministries. We do not use coaching notes or any bank's internal documents. Each question names its source.

2. A link you can click

More than 2,000 questions carry a "Check the source" link in the explanation. A link to an SBI page is added only if that page actually contains the figures in the answer.

3. Re-checked before every release

Before each update we re-read every live SBI page that our questions cite and compare each answer figure with the page as it stands today. In the check of 23 October 2026, 839 of 870 SBI answers were found exactly on the live page and 285 of 290 RBI answers; the rest were differences of wording, and each was checked by hand.

4. When a rule changes, we change the question

Banks and regulators revise limits and rates. When they do, we correct the question and show the change in the app's "What changed" list (also on our updates page), with what applied before and what applies now. Examples from October 2026: SBI's Lead Bank districts went from 275 to 281, and SBI's premium on retail non-callable deposits was cut; both were corrected within days. Questions whose official page was withdrawn, or where official sources disagreed, were removed rather than guessed.

5. Found a mistake? Tell us

Every question in the app has a "Report an error in this question" link, or write to nirvahlabs@gmail.com. We check every report against the official source.
