Clerk to officer promotion exam: how to prepare for the Merit / NCS and Trainee Officer channels

Many clerical staff are eligible for both channels. The topics overlap, so one preparation covers most of both; each channel then adds one thing of its own.

The two patterns

140 objective questions for 100 marks in 150 minutes. Marks per question differ by section; practical problems carry 2 marks each.

Trainee Officer

Section I: 120 objective questions for 80 marks in 120 minutes. Section II: one essay of about 250 words from three topics, 20 marks in 40 minutes, typed online. Overall 65% (60% for SC/ST); Section I 65% (60% for SC/ST); Section II 50% (45% for SC/ST).

What to do

Common to both channels

  • General banking, KYC and branch procedures carry the biggest share: your daily branch work is your best revision; learn the rule behind each step.
  • Products and services, digital channels and financial inclusion schemes: make a product table (who can use it, limits, key features).
  • CBS and IT security: learn the terms and the do's and don'ts of cyber safety.

Merit / NCS extra: practical problems

  • 10 questions carry 2 marks each: interest and TDS, drawing power and margin, cheque, note and locker rules, compensation.
  • Solve 5 worked problems every day until each takes under a minute.

Trainee Officer extra: passages and essay

  • Case passages: read the whole passage and choose the option that follows the rule and protects both customer and bank.
  • Essay: one 250-word essay a week on a banking topic: introduction, 3-4 short paragraphs, conclusion; typed within 40 minutes.

Last month

  • Two full mocks a week for your main channel, one for the other.
  • Revise figures, recent changes and the latest published results of the bank.

Frequently asked

Can I prepare for both channels together?

Yes. Most topics overlap; the Trainee Officer exam adds case passages and the essay, while the Merit exam adds practical problems.

What are the qualifying marks for the Trainee Officer exam?

Overall 65% (60% for SC/ST); Section I 65% (60% for SC/ST); Section II 50% (45% for SC/ST).

Which part carries the most marks per question?

In the Merit / NCS paper, practical problems in general banking: 10 questions of 2 marks each, 20 marks in all.
