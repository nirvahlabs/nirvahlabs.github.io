Exam and banking updates

Exam dates, pattern notes and the rule, limit and charge changes that matter for promotion exams: what applied before and what applies now. The same list appears in the Unnati app under "What changed", with practice questions on each change.

NPA & provisioningFrom 01 April 2027

Expected Credit Loss (ECL) provisioning starts

Before: Provisions on the incurred-loss IRAC basis. Now: Three-stage ECL framework; the 90-day NPA test stays. Official source ↗

Agri creditFrom 01 January 2027

New KCC Directions, 2026 apply to KCCs sanctioned from this date

Before: KCC limit on a 5-year basis, validity decided by banks. Now: Composite KCC with a 6-year tenure; 12/18-month crop seasons; Flexi KCC ₹10,000-50,000. Official source ↗

Deposits19 October 2026

SBI cuts premium on retail non-callable deposits

Before: Above card rate: +0.30% (1 year), +0.40% (2 years). Now: +0.15% (1 year), +0.20% (2 years) for ₹1 crore to below ₹3 crore. Official source ↗

Exam schedule16 October 2026

Promotion Year 2027-28 schedule of examinations

General cadre: MMGS-II to MMGS-III on 26 December 2026 (Saturday, afternoon); Clerical to JMGS-I, Merit and Normal-cum-Seniority channel, on 3 January 2027 (Sunday, morning); Clerical to JMGS-I, Trainee Officer channel, on 10 January 2027 (Sunday, morning); JMGS-I to MMGS-II on 17 January 2027 (Sunday, afternoon). Specialist cadre exams are on 9 and 24 January 2027.

Exam pattern16 October 2026

Official structure for Promotion Year 2027-28

MMGS-II to III: 140 objective questions (70 marks, 100 minutes) and a descriptive paper (30 marks, 80 minutes). JMGS-I to MMGS-II: 150 objective questions (75 marks, 120 minutes), including 20 on English comprehension. Clerical to JMGS-I: Merit / NCS 140 questions (100 marks, 150 minutes); Trainee Officer 120 questions plus an essay (100 marks, 150 minutes). See the syllabus pages for section-wise marks.

RBI policy16 October 2026

Daily CRR maintenance tightened

Before: At least 90% of required CRR daily. Now: At least 99% daily; ₹25,000 crore OMO sales announced. Official source ↗

Advances15 October 2026

SBI lending benchmarks raised after the repo hike

Before: RLLR 7.50% + CRP; EBLR 7.90% + CRP + BSP. Now: RLLR 7.75% + CRP; EBLR 8.15% + CRP + BSP. Official source ↗

Forex10 October 2026

Hedging without proving the exposure cut; no rebooking

Before: Up to USD 100 million without underlying documents. Now: USD 5 million across all ADs; cancelled INR contracts cannot be rebooked; FERR of 20% on contracts above $2 million. Official source ↗

RBI policy07 October 2026

First repo rate hike in about four years

Before: Repo 5.25%, stance neutral. Now: Repo 5.50%, stance "calibrated tightening"; FY27 GDP 7.1%, CPI 5.2%. Official source ↗

Forex01 October 2026

New FEM (Export and Import) Regulations, 2026

Before: Export realisation temporarily 15 months (Nov 2025 relief). Now: Realise and repatriate within 9 months; wider set-off of receivables against payables. Official source ↗

SBI charges01 October 2026

Free ATM transactions for SBI salary package accounts at other banks

Before: 10 a month (from 1 Dec 2025). Now: 5 a month (financial + non-financial); SBI ATMs stay unlimited. Official source ↗

SBI charges01 October 2026

SBI forex service charges revised

Before: Earlier schedule. Now: New schedule: export credit customers pay 0.10% p.a. upfront; import LC 0.08% a month; guarantees 1.20%/1.50% p.a.. Official source ↗

SBI charges01 October 2026

BSBD cash withdrawals beyond four a month are charged

Before: Branch withdrawals were not counted this way. Now: Four free cash withdrawals a month across all modes, then ₹15 + GST each; digital transfers stay free. Official source ↗

Digital18 September 2026

UPI person-to-person "collect requests" stopped

Before: P2P collect requests allowed. Now: Not permitted; collect only for verified merchants. Official source ↗

Economy10 August 2026

PM E-DRIVE outlay raised

Before: ₹10,900 crore. Now: ₹11,900 crore; target of about 72,000 public chargers unchanged.

SBI21 July 2026

SBI Funds Management listed

Before: Unlisted joint venture of SBI and Amundi. Now: Listed on BSE and NSE; the IPO was subscribed 41.66 times. Official source ↗

Grievances01 July 2026

RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2026 replaces the 2021 scheme

Before: Compensation up to ₹20 lakh + ₹1 lakh. Now: Up to ₹30 lakh for consequential loss + ₹3 lakh for time, expenses and harassment. Official source ↗

Credit information01 July 2026

Rejected credit data: new correction rule

Before: Earlier correction timeline. Now: Correct and re-submit before or with the next reporting date. Official source ↗

Forex18 June 2026

SNRR accounts opened up

Before: Only for non-residents with a business interest in India. Now: Any person resident outside India, for permissible transactions. Official source ↗

Laws26 May 2026

IBC Section 12A: withdrawal of an admitted application tightened

Before: Withdrawal with 90% of the CoC, at more stages. Now: Still 90% of the CoC, but applied through the resolution professional, and not before the CoC is formed or after plans are invited. Official source ↗

Economy13 May 2026

Customs duty on gold raised

Before: 6% (since July 2024). Now: 15% (10% basic + 5% AIDC). Official source ↗

Deposits01 April 2026

Form 121 replaces Forms 15G and 15H

Before: Form 15G (below 60) / 15H (60+). Now: A single Form No. 121 under the Income-tax Act, 2025. Official source ↗

Deposits01 April 2026

DICGC moves to risk-based premium

Before: Flat 12 paise per ₹100 for every bank. Now: Risk-based: 12 paise is the card rate, better-rated banks pay less. Official source ↗

Deposits01 April 2026

BSBD rule eased

Before: A BSBD holder could have no other SB account; existing one closed within 30 days. Now: Only a second BSBD account in the same bank is barred; the 30-day closure rule is gone. Official source ↗

MSME01 April 2026

Collateral-free MSE loans doubled

Before: No collateral up to ₹10 lakh. Now: No collateral up to ₹20 lakh (up to ₹25 lakh for good units, by Board policy). Official source ↗

Deposits01 April 2026

TDS on NRO interest moves to Section 393

Before: Section 195 of the Income-tax Act, 1961. Now: Section 393(2) of the Income-tax Act, 2025; "tax year" replaces previous year and assessment year. Official source ↗

HR01 April 2026

SBI Code of Ethics 3.0 in force

Before: Earlier version of the Code. Now: Version 3.0: reviewed every three years with Central Board approval; gift limits ₹1,500 (officers) and ₹500 (award staff). Official source ↗

SBI charges15 February 2026

SBI online IMPS charges revised

Before: Earlier slabs. Now: Free up to ₹25,000; ₹2 / ₹6 / ₹10 + GST for higher slabs; free for armed forces and Govt salary packages. Official source ↗

Forex09 February 2026

ECB limit under the automatic route revised

Before: Flat USD 750 million a year. Now: Higher of USD 1 billion outstanding or 300% of net worth. Official source ↗

SBI31 January 2026

SBI CHAKRA Centre of Excellence launched

Before: No dedicated centre for sunrise sectors. Now: CHAKRA, within the Project Finance & Structuring SBU, covering eight sunrise sectors. Official source ↗

Advances01 January 2026

New co-lending framework

Before: Bank-NBFC model; NBFC kept at least 20%. Now: Any bank/AIFI/NBFC pair, any loan; each partner keeps at least 10%. Official source ↗

Advances01 January 2026

SBI Policy on Penal Charges

Before: Earlier SBI penal-charge schedule (RBI's 2024 fair-lending rules). Now: Penal charges (not interest), capped at 5% p.a.; 2.40% p.a. up to 60 days, 5% beyond. Official source ↗

Digital31 December 2025

Payments Infrastructure Development Fund ended

Before: Subsidy for PoS terminals and QR codes in smaller centres (from 1 January 2021). Now: The fund's validity ended on 31 December 2025. Official source ↗

Customer service16 December 2025

SBI simplified deceased claims

Before: Older claim procedure and forms. Now: Without nomination, up to ₹15 lakh settled without surety; claims at any branch or online. Official source ↗

Laws15 December 2025

"Fortnight" for CRR/SLR redefined

Before: Saturday to alternate Friday. Now: 1st-15th and 16th-last day of each month. Official source ↗

SBI charges01 December 2025

Other-bank ATM charge for SBI savings accounts

Before: ₹21 + GST per cash withdrawal. Now: ₹23 + GST (non-financial ₹11 + GST). Official source ↗

Digital30 November 2025

SBI mCash discontinued

Before: Send money by mobile number/email with a passcode. Now: Use UPI, IMPS, NEFT or RTGS. Official source ↗

Deposits28 November 2025

Green deposits under RBI's Climate Finance Directions, 2025

Before: RBI's 2023 framework for green deposits. Now: Chapter III of the 2025 Directions; rupee deposits only; yearly third-party verification and impact assessment. Official source ↗

HR21 November 2025

Four Labour Codes in force

Before: 29 central labour laws. Now: Codes on Wages, Industrial Relations, Social Security and OSH. Official source ↗

Laws13 November 2025

RTI exemption for personal information reworded

Before: Exempt unless a larger public interest justified disclosure. Now: Section 8(1)(j) simply exempts information that relates to personal information (after the DPDP Act). Official source ↗

Deposits01 November 2025

New nomination rules (Banking Laws Amendment Act, 2025)

Before: One nominee per account. Now: Up to four nominees; simultaneous or successive for deposits, successive only for lockers. Official source ↗

Advances01 October 2025

RBI Project Finance Directions, 2025 take effect

Before: Earlier norms for projects under implementation. Now: New Directions apply; projects with financial closure before this date continue under the earlier norms. Official source ↗

Advances29 September 2025

Gold loans allowed for working capital of gold-using manufacturers

Before: No such carve-out. Now: Need-based working capital for manufacturers using gold as raw material; not for investment or speculation. Official source ↗

Forex01 September 2025

RBI Trade Relief Measures, 2025 for exporters

Before: Normal repayment and export-credit periods. Now: Moratorium from 1 September to 31 December 2025 for notified sectors; export credit up to 450 days for credit disbursed up to 31 March 2026. Official source ↗

Laws01 August 2025

Director's "substantial interest" threshold raised

Before: ₹5 lakh (since 1968). Now: ₹2 crore. Official source ↗

Laws01 August 2025

Co-operative bank directors: longer tenure

Before: Maximum 8 years. Now: Maximum 10 years (other than the chairperson and whole-time director). Official source ↗

SBI31 July 2025

SBI Project SARAL launched

Before: Branch-bound retail processes. Now: Re-engineered retail operations, including Universal Branch Access. Official source ↗

Digital09 May 2025

Payments Regulatory Board replaces the BPSS

Before: Board for Regulation and Supervision of Payment and Settlement Systems. Now: Payments Regulatory Board (PRB) under the PSS Act, 2007. Official source ↗

SBI charges01 May 2025

ATM decline charges waived for BSBD and KCC accounts

Before: ₹20 + GST for a decline due to insufficient balance. Now: No decline charge for BSBD and KCC accounts; KCC keeps unlimited free other-bank ATM use. Official source ↗

Banking structure01 May 2025

One State, One RRB amalgamation

Before: More than one Regional Rural Bank in several States. Now: One RRB per State; in Rajasthan, Rajasthan Gramin Bank (SBI-sponsored, head office Jaipur); SBI now sponsors nine RRBs. Official source ↗

Deposits21 April 2025

Minors may open and operate accounts on their own

Before: Bank-wise practice, usually through the guardian. Now: Minors above an age fixed by the bank (not below 10) may open and operate savings and term deposit accounts independently. Official source ↗

MSME01 April 2025

MSME definitions revised; CGTMSE cover raised

Before: Micro: investment ₹1 crore, turnover ₹5 crore; CGTMSE up to ₹5 crore. Now: Micro ₹2.5 crore / ₹10 crore; small ₹25 / ₹100 crore; medium ₹125 / ₹500 crore; CGTMSE up to ₹10 crore. Official source ↗

Advances01 April 2025

Priority Sector Lending Directions, 2025

Before: Education loans up to ₹20 lakh; 2020 Directions. Now: Education loans up to ₹25 lakh; loans to startups up to ₹50 crore qualify under MSME. Official source ↗

Govt schemes01 April 2025

Unified Pension Scheme begins

Before: National Pension System only. Now: Option within NPS: assured 50% of the average basic pay of the last 12 months, for 25 years of service.

Govt schemes31 March 2025
