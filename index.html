Why candidates struggle

The exam is not hard to understand. It is hard to prepare for.

Promotion candidates are working officers and staff. Time is short, material is scattered, and the descriptive paper worries many, especially those who think in Hindi.

The usual way

  • Notes from many places, some outdated after new RBI rules
  • No idea which sections carry the most marks
  • First timed mock happens in the exam hall
  • Descriptive answers written without structure or key words
  • Study only on weekends, then forget

Questions

Frequently asked

Which exams does Unnati cover?

MMGS-II to MMGS-III (objective and descriptive), JMGS-I to MMGS-II (objective with English comprehension), and Clerical to JMGS-I under both the Merit / Normal-cum-Seniority and the Trainee Officer channels (including the essay).

Where does the content come from?

Every question is written fresh from public sources such as RBI directions, Acts of Parliament, official scheme documents and published annual reports, and shows its source. Content is reviewed before release and updated when rules change.

Is it free?

The app is free to download and includes a free question set for each exam. A one-time purchase on Google Play unlocks the full content for your exam. There is no subscription.

Does it need internet?

No. Everything works offline. Internet is needed only to download the app, buy or restore a purchase, and get updates.

I write in Hindi. Will this help?

Yes. Menus can be shown in Hindi, and the writing toolkit gives key terms with Hindi meaning and common Hindi sentences in correct exam English.

Is my data shared?

No. There is no login, and your progress stays on your phone. See our privacy policy.

Is Unnati linked to any bank?

No. Unnati is an independent app by NIRVAH LABS and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any bank or examining body.
