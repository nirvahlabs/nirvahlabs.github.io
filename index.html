How to use Unnati

A simple plan from day 1 to exam day

Day 1 · 5 minutes

  • Install and choose your designation: clerk, Scale I or Scale II officer.
  • Set your exam date; the countdown and daily plan follow it.
  • Open Syllabus and see which sections carry the most marks.
  • Turn on the daily reminder and do your first 10 questions.

Every day · 20-40 minutes

  • Start with the 5-minute Daily Capsule, then finish today's plan.
  • Practise 10-20 new questions from one section; read the "Remember" line.
  • Clear yesterday's mistakes in Practice → Mistakes.
  • Write one descriptive answer and compare with the model.

Every week

  • One full mock in exam conditions, then open Marks Planner.
  • Read the result: practise your two weakest sections next week.
  • One Learn lesson for the descriptive paper, quoting a section from Write → Law.
  • Check "What changed" for new rules and limits.

Last month

  • Two full mocks a week; revise mistakes and starred questions daily.
  • Two rounds of Figure Blitz a day.
  • Every evening: one Fact Finder list (₹, days, %, age) and a Law Dictionary quiz (Write → Law).
  • Day before the exam: Fact Finder → "Last 24 hours".
  • Final week plan in the app for the exam-day checklist.

Frequently asked

Which exams does Unnati cover?

MMGS-II to MMGS-III (objective and descriptive), JMGS-I to MMGS-II (objective with English comprehension), and Clerical to JMGS-I under both the Merit / Normal-cum-Seniority and the Trainee Officer channels (including the essay).

Where does the content come from?

Every question is written fresh from public sources such as RBI directions, SBI product pages, Acts of Parliament, official scheme documents and published annual reports. It shows its source, and most answers have a link to the official page so you can check it yourself. Content is reviewed before release and updated when rules change.

₹1,699 for Scale II→III, ₹1,299 for Scale I→II and ₹999 for Clerical→Officer: a one-time payment on Google Play with lifetime access and all updates. No subscription. Diwali offer until 15 November 2026 with code DIWALI: ₹1,499 / ₹1,199 / ₹899.

Is there a written exam for Scale II to III this year?

Yes. The written examination for promotion from MMGS-II to MMGS-III is scheduled for 26 December 2026 (afternoon). Unnati's Scale II→III track follows the official pattern for that paper.

How do I know the answers are correct?

Every question is written from official sources (SBI, RBI, India Code, NABARD, SEBI and others), most carry a clickable source link, and all cited SBI pages are re-checked live before each update. When a rule changes, we correct the question and show the change. See how we check answers.

Are the questions in Hindi?

The questions are in English, as in the exam. Menus are available in Hindi, and the writing toolkit gives Hindi meanings of banking terms and Hindi-to-English help for the descriptive paper.

What if I find a mistake or have a doubt?

Tap "Report an error in this question" in the app, or write to nirvahlabs@gmail.com. We check every report against the official source.

The app is free to download and includes a free question set for each exam. A one-time purchase on Google Play unlocks the full content for your exam, with lifetime access: every update and every attempt until you are promoted. There is no subscription.

Does it need internet?

No. Everything works offline. Internet is needed only to download the app, buy or restore a purchase, and get updates.

I write in Hindi. Will this help?

Yes. Menus can be shown in Hindi, and the writing toolkit gives key terms with Hindi meaning and common Hindi sentences in correct exam English.

No. There is no login, and your progress stays on your phone. See our privacy policy.
