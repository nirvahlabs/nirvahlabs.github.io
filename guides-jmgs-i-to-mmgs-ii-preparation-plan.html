JMGS-I to MMGS-II promotion exam: preparation plan

The exam is on 17 January 2027: 150 objective questions, 75 marks, 120 minutes (about 48 seconds a question), with no descriptive paper. That makes it a pure speed-and-accuracy exam.

A 10-week plan

Weeks 1-3 · The 20-question sections

  • Deposit products, advance products, systems & procedures and English comprehension have 20 questions each: start there.
  • Every day: 20 questions from one section, explanations read, figures noted.
  • English comprehension: one passage a day, answering only from the passage.

Weeks 4-6 · The rest of the syllabus

  • Digital channels, government business, laws, macro-economy, forex and NPA management.
  • End of week 4: first full mock in 120 minutes.

Weeks 7-8 · Speed

  • One mock a week; note the questions that took longest.
  • Practise 10-minute bursts of 15 questions to hold the 48-second pace.

Weeks 9-10 · Revision

  • Two mocks a week. Mistakes book every day.
  • Last 5 days: figures, recent changes and English passages only.

Frequently asked

Is there a descriptive paper for Scale I to II?

No. The exam is objective only.

How do I prepare for the English comprehension section?

Read one passage a day and answer from the passage, not from outside knowledge. Learn the meaning of words from their context.

How fast do I need to be?

150 questions in 120 minutes is about 48 seconds a question, so practise in timed sets.
