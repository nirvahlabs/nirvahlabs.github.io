Last 30 days before the bank promotion exam: revision plan and exam-day checklist

In the last month, new topics give little; revision, mocks and fixing mistakes give most. This plan works for every promotion exam.

Days 30-22 · Mocks start

  • Two full mocks a week in exam conditions, at the same time of day as the exam.
  • After each mock: every wrong answer read, the rule noted, the question added to your mistakes list.

Days 21-8 · Close the gaps

  • Daily: your mistakes list first, then 20 questions from your weakest section.
  • Every evening: one list of figures (₹ limits, days, ages, rates, Act sections).
  • Read the recent rule changes once more; they are asked often.

Days 7-2 · Light and sharp

  • One mock every second day; short sessions only.
  • No new material. Figures, mistakes, recent changes.
  • Descriptive paper: write two timed answers to keep your speed.

Day before and exam day

  • Day before: a short revision of the hardest figures, then rest and sleep early.
  • Exam day: carry every document your call letter asks for, reach early, and follow the time plan you used in mocks.
  • Do not stay stuck: mark a hard question and return to it.

Frequently asked

Should I study new topics in the last month?

Only if a high-weight section is completely untouched. Otherwise, revision, mocks and fixing mistakes give more marks.

How many mocks in the last month?

About two a week, each followed by a careful review of every wrong answer.
