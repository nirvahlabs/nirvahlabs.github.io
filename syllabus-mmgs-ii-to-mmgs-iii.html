MMGS-II to MMGS-III promotion exam: syllabus, pattern and weightage

Topics and how to prepare

A section-by-section guide. Topics are indicative, based on the official section titles.

Product features: deposit / liability15 Q · 7.5 marks

  • Savings, current, term and recurring deposits; interest rules and TDS
  • KYC, nomination (up to four nominees), joint-account mandates
  • NRI accounts: NRE, NRO, FCNR(B), RFC
  • DICGC insurance, inoperative and unclaimed deposits, lockers
  • Special accounts: minors, HUF, trusts, companies, salary packages
How to prepare: Most questions ask a number: limit, tenor, age, rate or cover. Make a one-page list of figures and revise it weekly.

Product features: advances / assets15 Q · 7.5 marks

  • Retail loans: home, car, education, personal, gold, pension
  • Agriculture: Kisan Credit Card, priority sector targets
  • MSME loans: MUDRA, CGTMSE, collateral-free limits
  • Interest rate benchmarks: EBLR, MCLR; fair lending, KFS, penal charges
  • Securities: pledge, hypothecation, mortgage, guarantee
How to prepare: Learn eligibility, margin, maximum amount and repayment for each scheme, plus RBI caps such as LTV.

IT channels, payment solutions and new initiatives10 Q · 5 marks

  • UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS, NACH, BBPS, AePS
  • Cards: limits, contactless, tokenisation; e-mandates
  • Customer liability in unauthorised electronic transactions
  • Failed-transaction TAT and compensation
  • Digital lending, CBDC (e₹), Account Aggregator, cyber-fraud safety
How to prepare: Transaction limits and timelines are favourites. Revise the RBI customer-protection rules carefully.

Systems and procedures, organisation structure, HR20 Q · 10 marks

  • Account opening, KYC periodicity, deceased claims, dormant accounts
  • RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme and grievance redressal
  • Currency management: soiled notes, counterfeit notes
  • Banking Regulation Act sections, RBI powers, audit and compliance
  • HR: POSH, RTI, leave, conduct, vigilance
How to prepare: The biggest section. Combine branch experience with the rule behind each practice.

Forex / treasury / international banking5 Q · 2.5 marks

  • FEMA basics, LRS limit
  • Exchange rates, spot and forward, cross rates
  • Letters of credit (UCP 600), Incoterms
  • Money market, T-bills, call money, LAF
How to prepare: Only 5 questions. Learn the basics well rather than the details.

Government business, cross-selling, financial inclusion15 Q · 7.5 marks

  • PMJDY, PMJJBY, PMSBY, APY, NPS
  • PPF, SSA, SCSS, NSC, KVP, Sovereign Gold Bonds
  • Lead Bank Scheme, SLBC, RSETIs, business correspondents
  • Pension payment, government receipts, DBT
  • Insurance and mutual-fund distribution rules
How to prepare: Scheme numbers (premium, age, cover, maximum deposit) are asked often. Tabulate them.

Laws relevant to banks15 Q · 7.5 marks

  • Negotiable Instruments Act: Sections 6, 13, 85, 131, 138
  • SARFAESI Act, RDB Act (DRT), IBC
  • Indian Contract Act: guarantee, indemnity, bailment, lien
  • Transfer of Property Act: types of mortgage
  • PMLA, Limitation Act, Consumer Protection, DPDP Act
How to prepare: Link each section number to one fact. Expect questions on timelines such as notice periods.

Macro-economic environment / general awareness10 Q · 5 marks

  • Monetary policy: repo, MPC, CRR, SLR, inflation target
  • Fiscal terms: fiscal and primary deficit, FRBM, GST
  • Basel III capital, LCR, NSFR, D-SIBs, PCA
  • GDP concepts, balance of payments
  • Recent RBI and Government measures
How to prepare: Keep a monthly note of policy changes; the last six months matter most.

Pre/post-sanction processes and NPA management15 Q · 7.5 marks

  • NPA classification, SMA categories, provisioning
  • Wilful defaulters, frauds and red-flagged accounts
  • Appraisal ratios: current ratio, DSCR, TOL/TNW, break-even
  • Working capital: drawing power, MPBF, turnover method
  • Recovery: SARFAESI, DRT, Lok Adalat, compromise, ECL from 2027
How to prepare: Practise ratio and drawing-power calculations until they take under a minute.

Supervisory role and responsibilities10 Q · 5 marks

  • Branch controls: dual control, maker-checker, surprise checks
  • KYC/AML responsibilities, STR, CTR
  • Customer service, compensation, complaint handling
  • Staff matters, accountability and preventive vigilance
How to prepare: Questions are situational. Choose the option that follows the rule and protects both customer and bank.

Data interpretation10 Q · 5 marks

  • Tables and charts on banking data
  • Percentages, growth, ratios, averages, shares
How to prepare: Ten minutes of practice daily; speed comes from habit, not formulas.

A simple study plan

Every day (30-45 minutes)

One section of practice questions, plus your mistakes from earlier days.

Every week

One full mock in exam conditions; study the weakest two sections the following week.

Last month

Revise figures, limits and recent changes; take two mocks a week.

Structure as per the official guidelines for Promotion Year 2027-28. Always check the latest official notification.

Frequently asked

How many questions are in the MMGS-II to III objective paper?

140 questions carrying 0.5 marks each, 70 marks in all, to be answered in 100 minutes.

What is in the descriptive paper?

Rationale (4 of 6), For and against (4 of 6), Situational analysis (2 of 3), Comprehension (1 passage, 4 questions) and Business correspondence (1 of 2): 30 marks in 80 minutes, typed.

Can the descriptive paper be written in Hindi?

Rationale, For and against and Situational analysis can be written in English or Hindi. Comprehension and Business correspondence are in English only.

Which sections carry the most marks?

Systems and procedures (20 questions) is the largest; deposits, advances, government business, laws and NPA management carry 15 questions each.

Is there negative marking?

The published structure does not mention negative marking. Always confirm with the latest official notification.
