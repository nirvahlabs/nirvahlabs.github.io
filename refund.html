Purchase and refunds

Last updated: 25 October 2026.

Prices

One-time payment per exam on Google Play: Scale II → III ₹1,699 · Scale I → II ₹1,299 · Clerical → Officer ₹999. Offer prices, when running, are shown on the home page and in the app with their end date. The app and its free question set cost nothing. There is no subscription.

What "lifetime access" means

Your purchase unlocks the full content for the exam you bought, on the Google account used to buy it, for as long as Unnati is offered on Google Play. It includes every content update and new question for that exam, including updates when the exam pattern or banking rules change. Other exams are bought separately.

Restoring a purchase

On a new or reset phone, sign in with the same Google account, install Unnati and tap Restore purchase in the app. Study progress can be moved with the app's backup file or Android's own backup.

Refunds

Payments are processed by Google Play, so refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. You can request one from your Google Play order history or through Google Play support. If you face a problem with the content, write to nirvahlabs@gmail.com first; we will try to fix it or help with your request.
