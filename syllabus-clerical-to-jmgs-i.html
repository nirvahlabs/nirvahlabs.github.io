Clerical to JMGS-I promotion exam (Merit / NCS and Trainee Officer): syllabus, pattern and weightage

Clerical staff can be promoted to Junior Management Grade Scale I through the Merit and Normal-cum-Seniority channel (140 objective questions, 100 marks, 150 minutes) or the Trainee Officer channel (120 objective questions plus an essay, 100 marks, 150 minutes). Many candidates are eligible for both.

Exam date: 03 January 2027Promotion Year 2027-28Official structure

Two channels, two patterns

Merit / Normal-cum-Seniority · 3 January 2027

140 objective questions · 100 marks · 150 minutes · no essay

Trainee Officer · 10 January 2027

Section I: 120 questions · 80 marks · 120 minutes
Section II: essay (250 words, 1 of 3) · 20 marks · 40 minutes, typed
Qualifying 65% overall (60% SC/ST)

Merit / NCS: topics and how to prepare

General banking, KYC, legal issues, systems and procedures50 Q · 25 marks

  • Account opening, KYC and periodic updation
  • Cheques, clearing, NI Act basics
  • Deposits, nomination, deceased claims, lockers
  • Legal basics: minors, partnership, companies, garnishee
  • Branch procedures and customer service rules
How to prepare: Half the paper by questions. Your daily branch work is your best revision; learn the rule behind each step.

CBS and computer awareness, computer security20 Q · 15 marks

  • Core banking terms and processes
  • Computer basics, networks, internet
  • Passwords, phishing, malware, safe practices
  • Digital channels and their security
How to prepare: Know common terms and the do's and don'ts of cyber safety.

Products and services, associates and subsidiaries, other banks, alternate channels and IT products, financial inclusion30 Q · 15 marks

  • Deposit and loan products
  • Subsidiaries: life and general insurance, mutual fund, cards, capital markets
  • ATMs, mobile and internet banking, UPI, BC channel
  • Jan Dhan and social security schemes
How to prepare: Prepare a product table: who can use it, limits, key features.

Latest financial results, performance indicators, productivity and profitability10 Q · 5 marks

  • Latest quarterly and annual results
  • Deposits, advances, profit, NPA ratios, CASA
  • Productivity measures
How to prepare: Learn the latest published figures just before the exam.

Objective questions on practical problems in general banking10 Q · 20 marks

  • Interest and TDS calculations
  • Drawing power and margin
  • Cheque, note and locker rules
  • Compensation for failed transactions
How to prepare: Worth 20 marks for 10 questions. Practise worked problems every day.

General awareness10 Q · 10 marks

  • Banking and economy news
  • Government schemes, RBI policy
  • Important appointments and events
How to prepare: Revise the last six months of banking news.

Organisational structure and policy guidelines of the bank10 Q · 10 marks

  • Verticals, circles, regional offices and CPCs
  • Key published policies: customer rights, compensation, KYC
  • History and milestones
How to prepare: Read the bank's published policies and its annual report overview.

Trainee Officer: topics and how to prepare

General awareness about banking environment, KYC, legal issues20 Q · 10 marks

  • Banking structure and regulators
  • KYC and AML basics
  • Legal basics for bankers
How to prepare: Revise RBI basics and KYC rules.

General banking, handling of customer grievances and preventive vigilance (passages)20 Q · 20 marks

  • 10-15 line passages on branch situations, 5 questions each
  • Customer complaints and the Ombudsman
  • Dual control, job rotation, frauds, counterfeit notes
How to prepare: Read the whole passage. Pick the option that follows the rule and protects both customer and bank.

Products and services, retail finance, associates and subsidiaries, other banks, financial inclusion20 Q · 20 marks

  • Retail loans and deposits; USPs of products
  • Subsidiaries and their services
  • Financial inclusion schemes
How to prepare: Focus on what makes each product useful to a customer.

CBS and other IT products, IT security20 Q · 15 marks

  • Core banking and IT products
  • Cyber security and safe practices
How to prepare: Learn terms and security habits.

Alternate channel products20 Q · 10 marks

  • ATM, ADWM, internet and mobile banking, UPI
  • Business correspondents, POS, QR
How to prepare: Limits and features of each channel.

Latest financial results, performance indicators, productivity, organisational structure and history20 Q · 5 marks

  • Latest results and ratios
  • Organisation and history
How to prepare: Low marks per question; revise quickly near the exam.

Section II · Essay1 of 3 · 20 marks

  • Banking topics such as digital banking, financial inclusion, customer service, cyber fraud, KYC, MSME credit
  • About 250 words: introduction, 3-4 body paragraphs, conclusion
How to prepare: Write one essay a week against a 40-minute clock and compare it with a model essay.

A simple study plan

Every day (30-45 minutes)

One section of practice questions, plus your mistakes from earlier days.

Every week

One full mock in exam conditions; study the weakest two sections the following week.

Last month

Revise figures, limits and recent changes; take two mocks a week.

Structure as per the official guidelines for Promotion Year 2027-28. Always check the latest official notification.

Frequently asked

What is the pattern of the Merit / Normal-cum-Seniority channel exam?

140 objective questions for 100 marks in 150 minutes. Marks per question differ by section; practical problems carry 2 marks each.

What is the pattern of the Trainee Officer channel exam?

Section I: 120 objective questions for 80 marks in 120 minutes. Section II: one essay of about 250 words from three topics, 20 marks in 40 minutes, typed online.

What are the qualifying marks for the Trainee Officer exam?

Overall 65% (60% for SC/ST); Section I 65% (60% for SC/ST); Section II 50% (45% for SC/ST).

Can I prepare for both channels together?

Yes. Most topics overlap; the Trainee Officer exam adds case passages and the essay, while the Merit exam adds practical problems.
